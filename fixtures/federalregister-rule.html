Standards of Conduct; Revocation of Superseded Regulations; Revision of Residual Provisions

A Rule by the Health and Human Services Department on 08/22/2025

SUMMARY:

The Department of Health and Human Services (Department or HHS) is revising and reissuing the Standards of Conduct, a set of substantive and procedural rules relating to conduct and employee responsibilities that augment the Standards of Ethical Conduct for Employees of the Executive Branch.
